MATERIALS & STONES

Speak clearly
about what matters.

A future Lheureux catalog should identify every material and stone accurately, without vague luxury language.

GOLD

14K

Contains 58.3% pure gold. It is commonly chosen for a balance of gold content, strength and everyday wear.

GOLD

18K

Contains 75% pure gold. Its higher gold content gives a richer precious-metal composition while remaining suitable for fine jewelry.

DIAMONDS

Natural

Diamonds formed in the earth. Product listings should state carat weight, color, clarity and any grading documentation that applies.

DIAMONDS

Lab-Grown

Diamonds created in a controlled environment with the same crystal structure as mined diamonds. Listings should label them clearly.

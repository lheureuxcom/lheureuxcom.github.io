FROM REFERENCE TO LHEUREUX

What the maison can learn.

01

Entry Price

Simple 14K gold pieces create an accessible first step into the brand.

02

Specification Language

Metal, width, length, carat and stone type should be visible before purchase.

03

Good / Better / Best

A healthy assortment can range from minimal gold basics to higher-value diamond pieces.

04

Trust

Real materials, accurate dimensions and transparent service terms matter more than decorative marketing claims.

Use these references to decide which materials, stone types and price bands Lheureux should actually launch.

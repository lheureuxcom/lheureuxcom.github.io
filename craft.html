Lheureux is built around four design principles: proportion, light, restraint and identity.

01

Proportion

Balanced forms and deliberate scale create a sense of elegance before ornament is added.

02

Light

Surfaces, silhouettes and settings are imagined to interact with movement and changing light.

03

Restraint

Luxury is expressed through precision and simplicity rather than unnecessary excess.

04

Identity

A consistent visual language connects every ring, necklace, bracelet, earring and signature piece.
